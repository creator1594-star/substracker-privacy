Privacy Policy — SubTracker

Effective date: October 7, 2026

The short version

SubTracker works 100% on your device. We collect no subscription data, no personal data, no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone — there is nothing to sell, share, or leak, because we never receive it.

What the app does on your device

  • Stores the subscriptions you add (names, costs, billing cycles, dates) in a local database only on your phone.
  • Schedules local reminders (renewals, trial-ending countdowns) using Android's notification system — reminders are computed and fired entirely on-device.
  • The app requests no internet permission and includes no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • The notification permission is requested in context when you enable your first reminder; the app works fully if you decline it.

What we collect

Nothing. SubTracker:

  • requires no account and no sign-in;
  • sends no analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or device data to us or any third party;
  • shows no third-party ads;
  • does not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, files, or messages.

SubTracker Pro (in-app purchases)

SubTracker is free: track up to 10 subscriptions. SubTracker Pro unlocks unlimited subscriptions plus spending charts:

  • $4.99/month
  • $29.99/year
  • $14.99 one-time — lifetime access, no renewal

All purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service — we never see your card information.

Children

SubTracker collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy
